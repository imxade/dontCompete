**Types of Inventory**
========================

### Introduction
Inventory control is a crucial aspect of supply chain management that involves managing the flow of goods, materials, and products into, within, and out of an organization. Understanding different types of inventory is essential for effective inventory control.

### Core Concepts
In inventory control, there are three primary types of inventory:

*   **Raw Materials**: These are basic components or ingredients used in the production process.
*   **Work-in-Process (WIP) Inventory**: This type of inventory includes materials that have been partially processed but not yet completed.
*   **Finished Goods Inventory**: Completed products ready for sale or delivery.

### Examples with Solutions

Q1 (ID: me_2022-M_12):
Which one of the following is NOT a form of inventory?

(A) Raw materials
(B) Work-in-process materials
(C) Finished goods
(D) CNC Milling Machines

Solution:
The correct answer is (D). CNC Milling Machines are not considered a type of inventory. They are equipment used in manufacturing.

### Common Pitfalls
Common mistakes include:

*   Confusing raw materials with WIP inventory or finished goods.
*   Ignoring the context and understanding the question correctly.

### Quick Summary
*   Raw Materials: Basic components or ingredients used in production.
*   Work-in-Process (WIP) Inventory: Partially processed materials.
*   Finished Goods Inventory: Completed products ready for sale or delivery.

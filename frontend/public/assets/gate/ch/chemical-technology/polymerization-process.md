**Polymerization Process**
=========================

### Introduction

Polymerization is a chemical reaction where small molecules called monomers combine to form a large molecule called a polymer. This process is essential in the manufacturing of various materials, including plastics, rubber, and fibers.

### Core Concepts

*   **Monomers**: Small molecules that react with each other to form a polymer.
*   **Polymerization reactions**: Chemical reactions where monomers combine to form a polymer.
*   **Types of polymerization**:
    *   **Addition polymerization**: Monomers add to the growing polymer chain one at a time.
    *   **Condensation polymerization**: Monomers react with each other, forming a polymer and a byproduct such as water or methanol.

### Key Formulas/Theorems

$A_n + B_m → \text{Polymer}$

where $A_n$ is the monomer unit of molecular weight $n$, and $B_m$ is the monomer unit of molecular weight $m$.

### Problem Solving Patterns

When solving problems related to polymerization, consider the following:

*   Identify the type of polymerization reaction involved.
*   Determine the monomers used in the reaction.
*   Use the formula for polymerization reactions to relate the number of monomers to the degree of polymerization.

### Examples with Solutions

**Example 1**

Nitrile rubber is manufactured via polymerization process. Which one of the following options is the correct pair of monomers used in this process?

(A) Acrylonitrile and styrene
(B) Acrylonitrile and butadiene
(C) Butadiene and styrene
(D) Butadiene and isoprene

**Solution**

The correct answer is (B). Nitrile rubber is manufactured through the polymerization of acrylonitrile and butadiene.

### Common Pitfalls

*   Failing to identify the type of polymerization reaction involved.
*   Misidentifying the monomers used in the reaction.
*   Not considering the degree of polymerization.

### Quick Summary

*   Polymerization is a chemical reaction where small molecules (monomers) combine to form a large molecule (polymer).
*   There are two main types of polymerization reactions: addition and condensation.
*   The correct pair of monomers used in nitrile rubber production is acrylonitrile and butadiene.

**Oleum and its Components**
==========================

### Introduction
----------------

Oleum, also known as fuming sulfuric acid, is a highly corrosive and oxidizing mixture of sulfur trioxide (SO3) and sulfuric acid (H2SO4). It is widely used in various industrial processes, including the production of detergents, dyes, and other chemicals.

### Core Concepts
-----------------

#### Formation of Oleum

Oleum is formed through the reaction of sulfur trioxide with sulfuric acid:

$$\text{SO}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow 2\text{H}_2\text{S}_2\text{O}_7$$

This reaction occurs when sulfur trioxide is dissolved in concentrated sulfuric acid, resulting in the formation of oleum.

#### Composition of Oleum

The major components of oleum are:

* Sulfuric acid (H2SO4)
* Sulfur trioxide (SO3)

These two components make up approximately 95-98% of oleum's composition. The remaining 2-5% consists of water and other impurities.

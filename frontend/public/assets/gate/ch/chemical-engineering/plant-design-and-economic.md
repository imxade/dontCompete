**Plant Design and Economics**
============================

**Introduction**
---------------

Economic evaluation of process design is a critical aspect of chemical engineering, ensuring that investments are viable and profitable. This note covers key concepts, formulas, and problem-solving strategies for plant design and economics.

**Core Concepts**
-----------------

### Capital Investment

Capital investment (CI) is the total amount invested in a project, including initial costs such as equipment, land, and labor. The annual return on investment (ROI) is calculated using the formula:

$$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Annual profit}}{\text{Total capital investment}} \times 100$$

### Operating Costs

Operating costs (OC) are expenses incurred during the operation of a plant, including maintenance, labor, and utilities. The annual operating cost can be estimated using the formula:

$$\text{OC} = \text{CI} + (\text{ROI} \times \text{CI})$$

### Turnover Ratio

The turnover ratio (TOR) is a measure of how efficiently a plant utilizes its fixed capital investment. It is defined as:

$$\text{TOR} = \frac{\text{Gross annual sales}}{\text{Fixed capital investment}}$$

**Key Formulas/Theorems**
-------------------------

* Annual return on investment (ROI): $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Annual profit}}{\text{Total capital investment}} \times 100$$
* Operating costs: $$\text{OC} = \text{CI} + (\text{ROI} \times \text{CI})$$

**Problem Solving Patterns**
---------------------------

1. **Economic Evaluation**: Calculate the ROI, operating costs, and turnover ratio to determine the viability of a project.
2. **Capital Investment Calculation**: Estimate the total capital investment based on initial costs and equipment requirements.

**Examples with Solutions**
-------------------------

### Example 1: Economic Evaluation

A company invests Rs. 10 lakhs in a recovery unit to separate valuable metals from effluent streams. The recovered metals are worth Rs. 4 lakhs per year. If the annual return on investment is 15%, calculate the annual operating costs.

Solution:

* Total capital investment (CI) = Rs. 10 lakhs
* Annual profit = Rs. 4 lakhs
* ROI = 15% = 0.15
* Operating costs (OC) = CI + (ROI × CI)
= Rs. 10,00,000 + (0.15 × Rs. 10,00,000)
= Rs. 2,50,000

### Example 2: Turnover Ratio Calculation

A plant has a fixed capital investment of Rs. 5 crores and gross annual sales of Rs. 20 crores. Calculate the turnover ratio.

Solution:

* Fixed capital investment (FCI) = Rs. 5 crores
* Gross annual sales = Rs. 20 crores
* Turnover ratio (TOR) = $\frac{\text{Gross annual sales}}{\text{Fixed capital investment}}$
= $\frac{Rs. 20,00,00,000}{Rs. 5,00,00,000}$
= 4

**Common Pitfalls**
------------------

1. **Incorrect calculation of ROI**: Ensure that the annual profit and total capital investment are correctly calculated.
2. **Overlooking operating costs**: Calculate operating costs separately to ensure accurate economic evaluation.

**Quick Summary**
-----------------

* Capital investment (CI): Total amount invested in a project
* Operating costs (OC): Expenses incurred during plant operation
* Turnover ratio (TOR): Measure of efficiency utilizing fixed capital investment
* Annual return on investment (ROI): Profit divided by total capital investment

**Language Comprehension**
========================

### Introduction
---------------

Language comprehension is a crucial aspect of general aptitude, requiring the ability to understand and analyze written text. It involves identifying relationships between words, phrases, and sentences, as well as recognizing nuances in meaning.

### Core Concepts
-----------------

#### Prepositions and Conjunctions
-----------------------------------

*   **Prepositions**: Words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in the sentence.
    *   Examples: at, over, with, in, on, by, etc.
*   **Conjunctions**: Words that connect words, phrases, or clauses.
    *   Types:
        *   Coordinating conjunctions (and, but, or)
        *   Subordinating conjunctions (because, although, if)

#### Tenses and Aspects
-------------------------

*   **Tense** refers to the time at which an action occurs. Common tenses include past simple, present continuous, future perfect.
*   **Aspect** describes the duration or completion of an action.

#### Modifiers and Clauses
---------------------------

*   **Modifiers**: Words or phrases that provide additional information about a noun or pronoun.
    *   Examples: adjectives (big, happy), adverbs (quickly, wisely)
*   **Clauses**: Groups of words containing a subject and predicate.

### Key Formulas/Theorems
---------------------------

No specific formulas are required for language comprehension. However, understanding the rules governing grammar, syntax, and vocabulary is essential.

### Problem Solving Patterns
-----------------------------

1.  **Contextual Analysis**:
    *   Identify the relationships between words, phrases, or sentences.
    *   Recognize nuances in meaning based on context.
2.  **Grammar and Syntax Identification**:
    *   Determine the tense, aspect, mood, and voice of a verb.
    *   Identify types of clauses (independent, dependent) and conjunctions.

### Examples with Solutions
---------------------------

1.  **Example**: "The company will ____(i)__ the project by next week."
    *   Answer: **(i)** _complete_ or _finish_
    *   Explanation: The verb "will complete" indicates a future action, which is consistent with the present continuous tense.
2.  **Example**: "After working for hours, he ____(ii)__ exhausted."
    *   Answer: **(ii)** _was_
    *   Explanation: The adverb "exhausted" requires the past participle form of the verb "to be," which is "was."

### Common Pitfalls
-------------------

1.  **Misunderstanding Context**: Failing to recognize nuances in meaning based on context can lead to incorrect answers.
2.  **Incorrect Grammar and Syntax Identification**: Misidentifying tenses, aspects, or clauses can result in incorrect solutions.

### Quick Summary
----------------

*   Prepositions show relationships between words.
*   Conjunctions connect words or phrases.
*   Tense and aspect describe the time and duration of an action.
*   Modifiers provide additional information about nouns or pronouns.
*   Clauses contain a subject and predicate.

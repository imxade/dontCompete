**Verbal Ability - Sentence Completion**
======================================

**Introduction**
---------------

Sentence completion questions test a candidate's understanding of grammatical rules and conventions in English, particularly when expressing a statement in indirect speech. These questions assess the ability to convert direct speech into indirect speech, often requiring changes in verb tense, pronoun usage, or sentence structure.

**Core Concepts**
-----------------

When converting from direct to indirect speech, consider the following:

*   **Shift in Verb Tense**: Indirect speech typically uses the past perfect tense for reported statements that began before the time of speaking.
*   **Change in Pronouns**: The pronouns 'I' and 'you' become 'he/she/it' and 'they', respectively, when changing from direct to indirect speech.
*   **Subjunctive Mood**: Use the subjunctive mood for hypothetical or uncertain situations expressed in indirect speech.

**Key Formulas/Theorems**
-------------------------

While there are no specific formulas or theorems in this context, it's essential to understand and apply the above core concepts to solve sentence completion questions.

**Problem Solving Patterns**
---------------------------

When approaching a sentence completion question:

1.  Identify the original statement's verb tense and structure.
2.  Apply the conversion rules from direct to indirect speech, focusing on changes in verb tense, pronoun usage, and mood.
3.  Select the answer option that correctly represents the converted statement.

**Examples with Solutions**
---------------------------

### Example 1

Original Statement: "I am thinking of watching a film this weekend."

Indirect Speech Conversion:

*   Identify the original verb tense (present simple).
*   Apply the conversion rule for present simple to past simple in indirect speech.
*   Select the correct answer option: "Rafi told Mary that he **was** thinking of watching a film this weekend."

### Example 2

Original Statement: "You are watching TV right now."

Indirect Speech Conversion:

*   Identify the original verb tense (present simple).
*   Apply the conversion rule for present simple to past simple in indirect speech.
*   Select the correct answer option: "He told me that you **were** watching TV at that time."

### Example 3

Original Statement: "I will meet you tomorrow."

Indirect Speech Conversion:

*   Identify the original verb tense (future simple).
*   Apply the conversion rule for future simple to conditional or potential mood in indirect speech.

**Common Pitfalls**
------------------

Students often miss changes in pronoun usage, particularly when 'I' and 'you' are replaced by 'he/she/it' and 'they', respectively.

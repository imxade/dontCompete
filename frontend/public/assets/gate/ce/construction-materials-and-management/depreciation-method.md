**Depreciation Methods**
========================

### Introduction
---------------

Depreciation refers to the decrease in value of an asset over its useful life due to wear and tear, obsolescence, or other factors. It's a critical concept in accounting for construction materials and management.

### Core Concepts
-----------------

#### Straight Line Method (SLM)
The straight line method assumes that the asset loses its value uniformly over its useful life.

Mathematically, SLM can be represented as:

$$\text{Annual Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}$$

#### Double Declining Balance Method (DDBM)
The double declining balance method is an accelerated depreciation method, which assumes that the asset loses its value at a faster rate in the initial years.

Mathematically, DDBM can be represented as:

$$\text{Annual Depreciation} = 2 \times \left(\frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}\right) \times (1 - \frac{t}{\text{Useful Life}})$$

where $t$ is the current year.

### Key Formulas/Theorems
-------------------------

*   Straight Line Method: $\text{Annual Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}$
*   Double Declining Balance Method: $\text{Annual Depreciation} = 2 \times \left(\frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}}\right) \times (1 - \frac{t}{\text{Useful Life}})$

### Problem Solving Patterns
---------------------------

*   When comparing DDBM and SLM, always calculate the annual depreciation for both methods using their respective formulas.
*   In questions asking for the difference in depreciation between two years, use the formula for the year specified.

### Examples with Solutions
-------------------------

**Example 1**

A piece of equipment has an initial value of Rs. 1,00,000 and a salvage value of Rs. 10,000. Its useful life is 5 years.

Using SLM:

$$\text{Annual Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Salvage Value}}{\text{Useful Life}} = \frac{1,00,000 - 10,000}{5} = \text{Rs. }18,000$$

Using DDBM:

$$\text{Annual Depreciation} = 2 \times (18,000) \times \left(1 - \frac{t}{5}\right)$$

| Year | SLM | DDBM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1   | 18,000 | 36,000 |
| 2   | 18,000 | 27,600 |

**Example Solution**

The difference in depreciation between the first and second years is $36,000 - 18,000 = \boxed{Rs. 18,000}$ using SLM and $36,000 - 27,600 = \boxed{Rs. 8,400}$ using DDBM.

### Common Pitfalls
------------------

*   Misinterpreting the depreciation method.
*   Incorrectly applying formulas for specific years.

**Concrete Constituents Mix Design: Short-term and Long-term Properties**
====================================================================================

### Introduction
--------------------------------

Mix design for concrete is a crucial aspect of construction materials management. The process involves selecting the appropriate constituents (cement, aggregates, admixtures, etc.) in the right proportions to achieve the desired properties of fresh and hardened concrete.

### Core Concepts
------------------

*   **IS 456:2000**: This Indian Standard specifies the composition, specifications, and conformity criteria for different types of cement.
*   **pH Value**: pH is a measure of the acidity or alkalinity of water. Concrete mix design requires water with an appropriate pH level to ensure proper hydration of the cement paste.
